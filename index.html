01 SELF-ASSESSMENT

My Computer Science journey.

Completing my Computer Science degree has given me a foundation in software development, algorithms and data structures, databases, security, and problem solving. Throughout the program, I have learned how different areas of computer science work together to create functional and reliable software. Developing this ePortfolio gave me the opportunity to look back at that work, improve projects from earlier courses, and demonstrate how my skills have developed over time.

One of my strengths is being able to approach a problem from both a technical and practical perspective. My coursework has required me to analyze requirements, design solutions, write and test code, troubleshoot problems, and make changes based on feedback. My experience with the Travlr application gave me additional experience working with full-stack development and connecting the different parts of a web application. These projects helped me understand that successful software development involves more than writing code. It also requires organization, planning, communication, testing, and attention to the needs of the people using the software.

Collaboration and communication have also been important throughout my program. Working through project requirements and receiving feedback from instructors taught me how to explain technical decisions and make changes based on the needs of others. My experience outside of school as a freelance graphic designer has strengthened these skills as well. Working with clients requires me to understand their goals, ask questions, communicate progress, and turn their requirements into a finished product. This experience has helped me develop a user-focused approach that I can also apply to software development.

My coursework has also strengthened my understanding of data structures and algorithms. I have worked with structures such as hash tables and binary search trees and learned how the choice of a data structure can affect the performance of an application. I have also developed experience with software engineering concepts such as the software development life cycle, debugging, testing, and modifying existing applications. Database coursework has given me experience with storing, organizing, and retrieving information while considering relationships between data and application functionality.

Security has become another important part of how I approach software development. Through my coursework, I have learned to consider security when designing and modifying applications rather than treating it as something added at the end. This includes identifying weaknesses, validating user input, protecting user information, and considering how software could be misused.

My professional goal is to work in remote web design and development while combining my Computer Science education with my background in design. I value organization, creativity, problem solving, and creating technology that is useful and understandable to the people who use it. Completing the Computer Science program has given me the technical foundation to pursue that goal, while my freelance design experience has helped me develop the communication and creative skills that I can bring into a technical career.

Overall, the capstone and ePortfolio have helped me see the progress I have made throughout the program. Rather than only showing completed assignments, this portfolio demonstrates my ability to evaluate existing work, identify areas for improvement, apply new technical concepts, and explain the decisions behind my work. These skills will help me continue developing as a computer science professional.
